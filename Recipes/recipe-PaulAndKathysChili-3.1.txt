Paul n Kathy's Chili [v3.1 12/23/2007]  First Made 10/15/2003


This is a favorite recipe of ours.  We started with a very simple chili recipe
and tweaked it over several cookings.  It gets better with age, unlike most
leftovers.  Enjoy!  -p&k.


Spices Mix (single batch)
----------
____ 3   tsp  Chili Powder (or chili powder mix for hotter chili)
____ 1/2 tsp  Basil, dried, crushed
____ 1/4 tsp  Pepper
____ 1/2 tsp  Salt
____ 1   tsp  Cumin
____ 1/2 tsp  Red Pepper Flakes


Main Ingredients (single batch)
----------------
____ 1 lb       Ground Sirloin
____ 1 cup      Onion, chopped (1 large onion)
____ 1/2 cup    Sweet Green Pepper, chopped
____ 2 cloves   Garlic, minced
____ 1          Jalepeno pepper, diced (use seeds for hotter chili)
____ 14 oz can  Tomatoes (Tomato Puree, use Crushed if Puree not available)
____ 15 oz can  Dark Red Kidney Beans, drained
____ 8  oz can  Tomato Sauce


DIRECTIONS
----------
____ Mix spices together, set aside
____ In large pot, cook the following (until meat is brown/onion tender):
     ____ Ground Beef
     ____ Onion
     ____ Sweet Pepper
     ____ Garlic
____ Drain Fat (may not be necessary for Sirloin)
____ Stir in:
     ____ Tomatoes
     ____ Kidney Beans
     ____ Tomato Sauce
     ____ Jalepeno
     ____ Spice Mix
____ Bring to boil, reduce heat, cover
____ Simmer for 15mins, Stir occasionally
____ Check and add 1/2 tsp salt, if needed
____ Cook for additional 20-25mins, Serve.


Makes about 5 cups (4 main dish servings).  RECOMMEND:  DOUBLE BATCH
