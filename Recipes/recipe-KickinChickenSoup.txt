Kickin' Chicken Soup [v1.0 03/23/2008]  First Made in approx 2004


This is a spicy chicken soup that will knock your socks off.  It
also gets better with age (e.g. leftovers).  We know people who
actually make multiple batches of this soup and freeze it for later.


Main Ingredients (single batch)
----------------
____ 2 lg        Boneless Chicken Breasts
____ 3 tbsp      Canola Oil (can use Olive Oil instead)
____ 1 lg        Onion, chopped
____ 3 cloves    Garlic, minced
____ 1           Jalepeno Chili Pepper, minced, See Note
____ 32 oz       Low-Sodium Chicken Broth (1 carton)
____ 3 c         Fresh or frozen Corn kernels
____ 1 15 oz can Black Beans
____ 1           Lime, squeeze juice
____ 1 tsp       Salt (or to taste)
____ 1/2 tsp     Ground Red Pepper or Hot Sauce
____ 1/2 c       minced Cilantro
____ Freshly ground black pepper (to taste)
____ Shredded Monterey Jack Cheese
____ [OPTIONAL, but RECOMMENDED] Tortilla chips


NOTES
-----
- Need large stockpot
- Prep time approx 30 mins, Cook time approx 15 mina
- Include jalepeno seeds for more heat.  Recommended for guests: NO SEEDS


DIRECTIONS
----------
____ PREPARE CHICKEN
     ____ Heat broiler
     ____ Cook chicken, turning once after 5 mins, about 10 minutes total


____ PREPARE STOCK (can start when chicken is broiling)
     ____ Heat oil in stockpot over medium heat, add:
          ____ onion
          ____ garlic
          ____ jaleapeno (with or without seeds)
     ____ Cook until soft, about 3 minutes, add:
          ____ broth
          ____ corn (can be frozen)
          ____ beans
          ____ lime juice
          ____ salt
          ____ red and black pepper (to taste)
     ____ Heat to boil, reduce to simmer


____ Slice or shred cooked chicken
____ Add chicken and cilantro to pot
____ Heat for additional 5 minutes (we usually cook longer)
____ Serve in bowls and top each with cheese and crumbled tortilla chips


NUTRITION INFO
--------------
205 calories, 31% of calories from fat, 8g fat, 1.1g saturated fat, 10mg cholesterol
28g carbohydrates, 10g protein, 585mg sodium, 6g fiber


---
Makes approx.  4-6 main dish servings.  Leftovers from this dish are great!
