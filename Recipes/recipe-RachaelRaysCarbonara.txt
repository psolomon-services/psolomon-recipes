Rachael Ray's Carbonara [v1.1 03/22/2008]  First Made 12/31/2007


This is a really great Carbonara recipe, comparable to Pizza Docs in
Chicago.  Kathy and I think its better.


Main Ingredients (single batch)
----------------
____ 1 lb Pasta, Spaghetti or Rigatoni
____ 1/4 cup Extra Virgin Olive Oil
____ 1/4 lb Panchetta (Italian Bacon), chopped (small pieces)
____ 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes (original recipe had 1tsp, but it is too hot)
____ 5-6 cloves Garlic, chopped
____ 1/2 cup Dry White Wine (e.g. Chardonnay)
____ 1 cup Frozen Peas, defrosted
____ 2 large Egg Yolks
____ Freshly grated Romano Cheese
____ Handful of Fresh Flat Leaf Parsley, finely chopped, for garnish


NOTES
-----
- Start Water boiling first, start other activities when water is boiling and pasta
  is added.
- Save pasta water for later steps


DIRECTIONS
----------
____ LARGE SAUCEPAN:  Boil water, then add:
     ____ liberal amount of Salt
     ____ Pasta
____ Cook al dente (approx 8min), save cooking water for later step


START THESE NEXT STEPS WHEN WATER IS BOILING AND YOU ADD PASTA...


____ Defrost Frozen Peas


____ LARGE SKILLET:  Heat over medium heat, add:
     ____ Olive Oil
     ____ Pancetta
____ Brown Pancetta, 8 min, then add:
     ____ Red Pepper Flakes
     ____ Garlic
____ Cook 2-3 mins, then add:
     ____ Wine
___ Stir up all the pan drippings


___ SEPARATE BOWL:  Beat Egg Yolks, then add:
    ____ 1 large ladleful (approx 1/2 cup) of pasta cooking water (to temper eggs)


____ Drain Pasta
____ Add Pasta directly to skillet
____ Pour egg mixture over pasta
____ Add Peas
____ Toss rapidly to coat the pasta (without cooking egg)
____ Remove pan from heat, then add:
     ____ big handful of Cheese
     ____ lots of pepper
     ____ a little salt
____ Continue to toss (1-2 mins) until thickened
____ Garnish with parsley and extra grated Romano Cheese


---
Makes approx.  4 main dish servings.
