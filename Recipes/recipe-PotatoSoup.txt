Yummy Potato Soup.


Main Ingredients (single batch)
----------------
____ 6-7 medium Potatoes, peeled, cut into bite-sized pieces
____ 1 Onion (or 2 Leeks), chopped
____ 2-3 Carrots, peeled and chopped (small pieces)
____ 6 small Chicken Bullion Cubes (or 4 big cubes, or 5 cubs Chicken Broth)
____ 1 tbsp Parsley Flakes (or 2tbsp fresh leaves, diced)
____ 5 cups Water
____ 1/4 tsp Pepper
____ 1/3 cup Butter (or Margarine)
____ 13 ounces Evaporated Milk (can substitute regular)


NOTES
-----
- If you use Chicken Broth, do not use the Water


DIRECTIONS
----------
____ Place all ingredients, except Milk, in crockpot
     ____ Water
     ____ Potatoes
     ____ Onion or Leeks
     ____ Carrots
     ____ Bullion Cubes
     ____ Parsley
     ____ Pepper
     ____ Butter
____ Cook on LOW for 10-12 hours, or HIGH for 3-4 hours
____ About 1 1/2 hours before serving:
     ____ mash up Potatoes (somewhat)
     ____ add milk
____ Heat thouroughly again in crockpot
____ Salt and Pepper to taste


Fixins:


____ Bacon Bits
____ Grated Cheddar Cheese
____ Crusty Bread


---
Makes approx.  6 main dish servings.
