Penne al Sugo di Carne [v1.0a 04/02/2008]  First Made 03/23/2008
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is a really great pasta recipe, more like a goulash than a pasta
sauce.  It is so good, you won't want to stop eating it!


Main Ingredients (single batch)
----------------
____ 5 tbsp   Butter (divided into 1 tbsp cubes)
____ 2 1/2 lb Boneless Chuck Roast cut into 2 inch cubes, sprinkled with salt and pepper
____ 2 med    Red Onions, sliced thin (use slicer if you have one)
____ 1/2      Bottle fruity red wine (e.g. Pinot Noir, SAVE ADDITIONAL WINE FOR END,
              ABOUT 1 CUP)
____ 1 28oz   Chopped Tomatoes (in juice -- keep juice for recipe)
____ 1/2 c    Brewed Espresso (can be brewed ahead of time and refrigerated)
____ 1 lb     Penne pasta (1 box)
____ 1 can    Beef Broth (probably need 1/2 - 1/3 of it at the end of the recipe)
____ 1/3 c    Pecorino Romano (or other) Cheese, coarsely grated (can also use
              Kraft Mediterranean Crumbles)


NOTES
-----
- Cook time for this recipe is over 2 1/2 hours, so keep that in mind!
- Need large Oven-proof pot with lid
- You can make this dish up to 3 days ahead.  Chill beef mixture uncovered until cold,
  then cover and keep chilled.  Rewarm before serving.


DIRECTIONS
----------
____ Preheat Oven to 400 degrees
____ Brew Espresso
____ Melt 1 tbsp butter in large oven-proof pot over medium heat
____ Sprinkle beef cubes with salt and pepper
____ Brown beef cubes, work in batches, about 4 mins per batch
____ Transfer beef to large bowl
____ Reduce heat to medium
____ Add remaining butter and saute onions until soft (approx 5min), stirring
     frequently
____ Return beef (and all juices) to pot, and add:
     ____ wine (1/2 bottle)
     ____ can of tomatoes (including tomato juice)
     ____ espresso (1/2 c)
____ Bring to boil, cover, transfer to oven
____ Braise beef in oven until tender (about 2 hours)
____ Start water boiling about 15 minutes before beef is done
____ Take beef pot out, using 2 forks, shred beef in pot
____ Add additional wine (about 1 c) and beef broth (1/3/- 1/2 can) for desired sauce
     consistency, stir
____ Put lid on beef pot back and place back in oven for 15 more minutes
____ While Beef is back in oven, heat water and cook pasta, al dente (tender but firm
     to bite)
____ Place pasta in bowls and spoon meat sauce over posta, top with small amount of
     cheese


---
Makes approx.  4-6 main dish servings.  Leftovers from this dish are great!
