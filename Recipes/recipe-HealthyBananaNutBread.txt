Very tasty, and healthy Banana Nut Bread


Main Ingredients (single batch)
----------------
____ 1 cup Sugar
____ 1/4 lb. Butter (one stick)
____ 2 Eggs
____ 2 cups Flour (1/2 white, 1/2 whole wheat)
____ 1 tsp Baking Soda
____ [OPTIONAL] 1 cup Chopped Nuts (pecans or walnuts)
____ 3 tbsp Ground Flax Seed
____ 3 Bananas, mashed


NOTES
-----
- For smaller muffins, bake 20-30 minutes (we use 25 mins)


DIRECTIONS
----------
____ Preheat oven to 350 degrees
____ Cream Butter and Sugar together
____ Add Eggs and beat
____ Add and Stir in:
     ____ Flour
     ____ Baking Soda
     ____ Chopped Nuts
     ____ Flax Seed
____ Add and stir in Mashed Bananas
____ Pour into well-greased loaf pan (or muffin pan)
____ Bake at 350 for 60-80 mins (we use 53-55 minutes for perfect)
